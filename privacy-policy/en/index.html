1. Core Principles

Starpath is a self-reflection app that helps users write down one problem, meditate, answer eight guided reflection questions, and light one star at a time.

Starpath follows these core privacy principles:

  • Problems and reflection answers written by users are stored locally on the user's device by default.
  • The developer does not collect users' problem text, reflection answers, or meditation record contents on a developer-operated server.
  • Starpath does not require account registration.
  • Starpath does not use users' reflection contents for ad requests, analytics events, or marketing purposes.

3. Problems and Reflection Records Written by Users

Problems and reflection answers written in Starpath may be highly personal.

Starpath currently does not operate its own server and does not transmit users' problems or reflection answers to a developer server. These records are stored in the app's local storage and may be deleted from the device if the user deletes the app or resets app data.

If a user exports data through the backup feature, the backup file is moved to the sharing or storage location selected by the user. The user is responsible for how that backup file is stored, shared, or deleted.

4. Advertising

Starpath may show interstitial ads to free users after they complete one star reflection cycle. Starpath is designed not to show ads while the user is writing a problem, meditating, or writing reflection answers.

Starpath may use the Google AdMob SDK to provide ads. Google AdMob may process device information, advertising identifiers, app information, and approximate usage information for ad delivery, ad performance measurement, and fraud prevention.

Starpath does not include users' problem text or reflection answers in ad requests.

For more information about Google's data processing, please refer to Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

5. In-App Purchases

Starpath may offer a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads.

Product ID: starpath_plus_ad_free

Payments are processed through Apple App Store or Google Play billing systems. Starpath does not collect or store direct payment method information such as credit card numbers or bank account details.

The app may store the purchased product ID and purchase check time locally on the user's device to apply ad-free status.

6. Notifications

Starpath may send daily reflection reminder notifications only if the user turns reminders on.

Notification permissions can be changed at any time in the operating system settings. Turning off notifications does not prevent users from using Starpath's core features.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Starpath's reflection records and app settings are stored locally on the user's device by default.

Users can delete data in the following ways:

  • Use the in-app data reset feature
  • Delete an individual star record
  • Delete the app
  • Delete app data through operating system settings

If a backup file has been saved or shared outside the app, the user must delete that file directly.

9. Children's Privacy

Starpath is not designed specifically for children. Because the app's self-reflection features may involve personal problems and emotions, minors are encouraged to use the app with guidance from a parent or guardian.

If you believe Starpath has intentionally collected personal information from a child, please contact starpath.official.app@gmail.com.

10. Important Notice for Sensitive Situations

Starpath is an app that supports meditation and self-reflection. It does not provide medical services, mental health diagnosis, treatment, counseling, or professional advice.

If a user is experiencing severe depression, self-harm urges, extreme anxiety, risk of violence, or any urgent situation requiring professional help, they should immediately contact local emergency services, a medical provider, a mental health professional, or a trusted person.

11. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes, Starpath may provide notice through the app or the public policy page. For important changes, Starpath will use an appropriate method to notify users.
